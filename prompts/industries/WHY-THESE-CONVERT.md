## Live RizzDial evidence (solar)

A 2025 solar full-qual setter transferred on live calls that ran about 4 to 5 minutes. The pattern that transferred was not a product dump and not a government pretext.

What actually moved the live human:

- Confirm the name first (`Hey {{first_name}}?`)
- How are you doing today in the same breath as the reason (opt-in) or, on cold, name plus how-are-you then the {{utility}} bill in {{city}}
- Soft energy check (bill going up, not a panel lecture)
- Permission, then book a free 10-minute roof and bill analysis
- Light quals after the sit is booked. Max 3. Disqualify renters fast
- Hang-tight transfer to an energy specialist who would run numbers and see if they actually qualify

Volume later died from voicemail and connection, not from that live script. Talking into a mailbox is not a win. If AMD or voicemail is detected, hang up. Do not leave a message. Rank by live transfer and a booked time, never by a "successful call" flag that marks voicemail talk as a win.

What that setter also proved, and what this pack keeps:

- Transfer-when-hot is a conversion. Do not grind a ready homeowner into an eight-question survey.
- Cap discovery at 3 quals, then book or transfer. Gong 11 to 14 questions is for a later discovery, not a 90-second setter.
- Timing objections (busy, pregnant, kids, dinner) get respect and a later callback, not a grind.
- Cost objections do not get an argument. Pivot to bill math and lease or PPA versus purchase.

What this pack drops on purpose:

- Pretending to be a state or federal "net metering program"
- "Your property popped up on our system" as if a government file pulled their data
- Promising the 30 percent federal residential tax credit on a customer-owned system (Section 25D ended for installations completed after December 31, 2025)

## 2026 industry research (solar)

Post-ITC residential solar is a bill conversation, not a credit conversation.

- Lead with the electric bill and local rate hikes, not credits.
- If they say the federal credit is gone for purchased systems, agree. That is a credibility move. Then talk bill plus verified `{{state}}` programs only. Never invent New York or Illinois numbers as always-true.
- Lease and PPA can have different economics. A credit may sit with the financier under 48E. Never tell the homeowner they get a federal credit.
- Cold openers: do not fake warmth. Name plus how-are-you in one breath, then the {{utility}} bill in {{city}}. Utility plus neighborhood, not a state program.
- Sell a free 10-minute roof and bill analysis, not panels. Book the sit, then light quals.
- Two-slot close. Never "when works for you." Objection pattern: agree, shrink the ask, two named slots.
- Max 3 quals, not 11 to 14. Gong-style discovery is not a setter call.
- Battery only if they ask. CA HIS/C-46 and TX SB 1036 (registration September 1, 2026) live in Critical Instructions as placeholders, not spoken lines.
- Transfer when they are hot and a human is on the floor.
- TCPA and DNC: if they say do not call, confirm and end. No legal advice.

## Cross-industry converting rules (this pack)

Every file here uses the same voice, inbound and outbound:

- Opt-in: ~"How are you doing today?" is one beat in the same breath as identity, AI disclosure, and the form or ad reason. Never stack "is this a good time?"
- Cold solar: name plus how-are-you in one breath, then the bill. Do not fake warmth.
- AI disclosure as capability plus a human path, one sentence. Never deny being AI. Never dump a speech.
- Book if the calendar is open. Transfer if a human is available and the lead is hot. Do not grind. Sub-2-minute if they are ready.
- One question at a time. Two named slots. Time Contract. Permission Close. Takeaway.
- If the calendar is empty, transfer if a human is available, else offer two named slots the team uses most.
- If AMD or voicemail, hang up.

Industry-specific notes, no real client names:

- **Opt-in speed-to-lead.** Call within minutes of the form. Name `{{lead_source}}`. They already asked. Finish the book or transfer.
- **Insurance.** AI must not quote or claim a license. Auto/home: coverage type, carrier, renewal. Medicare: no unsolicited MA/Part D, no benefit talk, SOA 48h wait through September 30, 2026, then verify CMS.
- **Real estate ISA.** Fair Housing. 3 quals max: buy/sell, 30/60/90, already have an agent. No invented CMA.
- **HVAC.** Intake, not pitch. ZIP, age or symptom, two slots. Transfer on gas, CO, or no heat.
- **Roofing.** Sell the 15-minute inspection. Inspection is not a claim. Transfer if an adjuster is on site.
- **B2B SaaS and agency.** Permission, one trigger, calendar-first two-slot. No mini-demo. No ROAS guarantee.
- **Senior living.** Buyer is often the adult child. Book the tour in the next 72 hours. Transfer on clinical or safety. No fake scarcity.
- **Credit and debt.** Consult only. Do not enroll or take a fee (CROA/TSR). No score promises.
- **Legal / PI.** Empathy, contact, date/treatment/represented. Never value the case. "How did you hear about us?" is not the first 30 seconds.
- **Mortgage.** No rate quotes (SAFE Act). NMLS placeholder. Do not use the opt-in script on trigger leads.
- **Automotive.** Do not re-qualify a named vehicle. Transfer on credit, negative equity, or exact price.
- **Fitness, education, restaurant, beauty.** Book the visit, tour, class, reservation, or chair. Two slots. Transfer when hot.

## How to use this folder

1. Pick the file that matches the traffic (inbound, outbound opt-in, or solar cold).
2. Swap `{{company_name}}`, `{{agent_name}}`, `{{offer}}`, hours, and calendar functions if your stack is not GHL.
3. Keep the 12 sections in order. Keep the same-breath how-are-you on opt-in. Keep AI disclosure as capability plus a human path.
4. If you add a fourth qualification question on a voice call, you left the converting pattern.

The generated verticals in `prompts/*.md` stay as the engine output. This folder is the hand-built converting pack.
